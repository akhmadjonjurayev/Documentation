1. Indekslash va slicing: 
Indekslash - ma'lum bir elementni olish. Slicing - massivning bir qismini olish: a[start:stop] yozuvida stop indexsining o'zi olinmaydi
a[0] — birinchi element.
a[-1] — oxirgi element.
a[1:4] — 1-indeksdan 4-indeksgacha, 4 kirmaydi.
a[::2] — har ikkinchi element.

2D massiv
m = np.array([
    [10, 20, 30],
    [40, 50, 60],
    [70, 80, 90]
])

print(m[0, 1])  # 1-qator, 2-ustun
print(m[1, :])  # ikkinchi qatorning hammasi
print(m[:, 0])  # birinchi ustunning hammasi
print(m[:2, 1:])  # dastlabki 2 qator, 2-ustundan boshlab

a = np.arange(1, 7)

Chiziqli tenglamani yechish usuli:
# 2x + y = 8
# x + 3y = 13

A = np.array([
    [2, 1],
    [1, 3]
])

b = np.array([8, 13])

solution = np.linalg.solve(A, b)

print(solution) # [2.2, 3.6] demak x=2.2 y=3.6
